Project Statement:

Kenyan smallholder farmers and everyday buyers experience a trust gap in farm
trade because online or informal sales often lack reliable payment handling,
seller verification, and clear delivery accountability. Farmers struggle to
reach paying customers and get paid on time; buyers struggle to know who is
legitimate and whether payment is safe. This makes it difficult for communities
to scale fair, repeat trade beyond informal networks. We want to explore a
solution that helps farmers list verified products and helps buyers purchase
with confidence, while Mercy Gold holds payment until delivery is confirmed.

================================================================================
3. TARGET USERS & COMMUNITY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

Primary Users:
  • Smallholder and small-scale farmers (honey, milk, eggs, produce, and related
    farm goods) who need fair market access and predictable payouts.
  • Household and small-business buyers seeking fresh, local farm products.

Secondary Users:
  • Platform operators / admins who verify sellers, approve listings, and
    coordinate payment release after delivery.

Key User Needs:
  • Clear separation of customer vs farmer roles so tools match real intent.
  • Admin verification of farmers after outreach (legitimacy before listing).
  • Category-based shopping (honey, milk, dairy, produce, other) without
    confusing empty categories with the wrong products.
  • Platform-held payment and payout visibility after delivery confirmation.
  • Transparent terms and consent for data use under Kenyan expectations
    (including Data Protection Act 2019 principles).

================================================================================
4. PROPOSED SOLUTION & KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

Mercy Gold is a web marketplace where Mercy Gold acts as a trusted middleman:
buyers shop farm products; farmers list after verification; the platform
collects payment and aims to pay farmers only after delivery is confirmed.

Core Feature 1 (Demonstrable prototype):
  Multi-role marketplace — customer storefront, farmer console (/sell), and
  admin console (/nas) with role gates so customers cannot access seller tools.

Core Feature 2:
  Trust workflow — farmer self-signup with location and goods → admin
  verify/activate → product approval before public listing → order line items
  tagged by seller → payout status after delivered.

Core Feature 3:
  Human-centered commerce UX — category filters with honest “no stock yet”
  states, Our Story / FAQ / Terms, guest checkout option, and secured-checkout
  messaging focused on people and process, not technology for its own sake.

================================================================================
5. ROLE OF TECHNOLOGY & AI (PEOPLE FIRST, AI SECOND)
================================================================================

Why this technology?
  A practical web stack (Next.js storefront + FastAPI API + PostgreSQL) matches
  how communities already use phones and browsers. The product value is the
  trust process (verification, approval, payment-after-delivery), not novelty
  infrastructure.

Role of AI:
  AI is not required for the core trust loop. If AI is used later, it should
  remain optional and secondary (for example, draft listing descriptions or
  help-desk replies) under human admin review. People—farmers, buyers, and
  operators—remain the decision-makers for verification, approval, and payout.

================================================================================
6. TRUST, SAFETY, AND DATA PRIVACY
================================================================================

Source & role transparency:
  Listings and flows distinguish platform products vs farmer products; status
  badges show pending vs approved; seller accounts show pending verification.

Verification & human oversight:
  Admins verify farmers after contact, approve or reject products, and control
  payout readiness only after delivery status is set.

Data minimization & purpose:
  Registration and seller fields collect what is needed to trade and verify
  (contact, location, goods, M-Pesa details for payout). Terms require explicit
  acceptance and describe use for verification, security, orders, and service
  improvement, aligned with principles of Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

Safety controls:
  Inactive/unverified sellers cannot create listings; their products stay hidden
  from the public shop.

8. POTENTIAL LIMITATIONS & FUTURE SCOPE
================================================================================

Current Limitations:
  • M-Pesa may be recorded as a payment method without full STK Push / webhook
    automation in the prototype.
  • SEO is basic (metadata, sitemap); not yet a full growth-SEO system.
  • Payout to farmers is operational (status flags) rather than automated
    disbursement rails.

Future Roadmap:
  • Full M-Pesa integration (STK Push / C2B, webhooks, auto status updates).
  • Stronger SEO (structured data, OG tags, performance, category landing pages).
  • Richer farmer tools (notifications, clearer payout statements) and deeper
    dispute-handling workflows while keeping humans in control.
